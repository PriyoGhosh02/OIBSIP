## ✨ Overview

**Antix** is a vibrant, modern landing page designed for a premium artisanal ice cream shop. Built with clean semantic HTML5 and vanilla CSS3, it showcases small-batch, 100% natural ice creams with playful typography, vibrant color palettes, smooth hover animations, glassmorphic badges, and a mobile-first responsive layout.

Developed as part of the **Oasis Infobyte (OIBSIP) Web Development and Designing Internship (Level 1 Task: Landing Page)**.

## 🎨 Key Features & Sections

### 1. 🧭 Sticky Modern Navigation Bar
- **Custom Brand Logo**: Distinctive `Antix` brand with customized pink dot over the `i` and pink `x`.
- **Navigation Links**: Home, About Us, Flavors, Shop, Reviews, and Contact.
- **Header Actions**: Interactive search button, shopping cart badge with live item counter, and a gradient pill **Order Now** CTA.

### 2. 🍧 Hero Section ("Scoop. Smile. Repeat!")
- High-impact headline with playful tri-color typography (`SCOOP. SMILE. REPEAT!`).
- **Feature Badges**: 100% Natural Ingredients, No Artificial Colors/Flavors, and Made with Love.
- **Rating Card**: Floating glassmorphic rating badge showcasing *4.9 ★★★★★ from 2K+ happy customers*.

### 3. 🍓 Flavors Carousel ("Our Delicious Creations")
- Interactive flavor showcase highlighting handcrafted flavors: *Salted Caramel, Classic Vanilla, Chocolate Fudge, Pistachio Bliss,* and *Strawberry Burst*.
- Circular halo cards with zoom-on-hover micro-interactions, carousel controls, and active pagination indicators.

### 4. 🎁 Special Offer & Discount Promo Section
- **Left Promo Card ("SWEET DELIGHT!")**: Turquoise card with chocolate-dipped ice cream bar artwork (`icecreame.jpeg`), special offer tag, description, and white pill "Shop Now ➔" button.
- **Right Discount Card ("20% DISCOUNT")**: Soft blush pink card displaying price comparison discounts for *Fresh Strawberry ($3.99)*, *Belgian Chocolate ($3.99)*, and *Classic Vanilla ($3.99)* with full-width "View All Deals ➔" button.

### 5. ⭐ Core Value Badges
- 4-column feature highlights: *Natural Ingredients, Handcrafted, Fast Delivery,* and *Eco Friendly*.

### 6. 📖 Our Story (About Us)
- Split brand story card highlighting passion, artisanal recipes, and local kitchen roots alongside team imagery.

### 7. 💬 Testimonials Slider ("Loved by Thousands")
- Customer reviews with 5-star ratings, reviewer avatars, quotes, and slider controls.

### 8. 📸 Community Gallery & Stats ("Shared With Our Community")
- **Teal Banner Card**: Two-column header with "SWEET MOMENTS" eyebrow, description, and "Join Our Community ➔" CTA button.
- **5-Column Photo Grid**: Curated customer and pet community moments with frosted glassmorphic like counter badges (`12.4K`, `8.7K`, `15.2K`, `9.8K`, `11.6K`).
- **Community Stats Bar**: 4 key metrics with pastel circular icons:
  - 👥 **50K+** Happy Customers
  - 📷 **10K+** Community Posts
  - 💛 **4.9/5** Average Rating
  - ⭐ **100+** Sweet Moments Daily

### 9. 💌 Newsletter Banner ("Stay Sweet with Antix!")
- Creamy peach card with "STAY SWEET" eyebrow and email subscription form.
- **3 Perk Badges**: *Exclusive Offers*, *New Flavor Alerts*, and *Sweet Inspiration*.
- Right-side visual artwork of an artisanal waffle bowl with scoops (`newsletter.jpeg`).

### 10. 🍨 Themed Footer
- Background decorated with `footer bg.png` featuring strawberry and chocolate chip scoops.
- Antix brand overview, Quick Links, Help & Support, 4 pastel brand-tinted social buttons (Instagram, Facebook, Twitter, YouTube), and copyright bar.

## 💻 Getting Started Locally

To run this project locally on your machine:

1. **Clone the repository:**
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/PriyoGhosh02/OIBSIP.git
   ```

2. **Navigate into the project directory:**
   ```bash
   cd OIBSIP/WebDev-L1-LandingPage
   ```

3. **Open `index.html` in your browser:**
   - Double-click `index.html` to open it directly in any web browser, OR
   - Run a local server:
     ```bash
     # Using Python 3
     python -m http.server 8000
     ```
   - Visit `http://localhost:8000` in your browser.
